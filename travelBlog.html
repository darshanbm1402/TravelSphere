Places to visit in INDIA

Popular destinations in India include the Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur) for history and iconic landmarks like the Taj Mahal; the serene backwaters of Kerala and lush hills of Munnar for nature lovers; the tranquil beaches and vibrant culture of Goa; and the majestic, snow-capped mountains of Leh-Ladakh.
